A little clarity

Good questions.
Straight answers.

This is a preview of the TireLane experience. Here is what works now and what comes next.

Can I buy tires here yet?

Not yet. This is a design and interaction demo. No orders, payments, reservations or bookings are created.

Does the search confirm fitment?

No. It filters a small example dataset by the displayed size and season. A production fitment service must also verify vehicle configuration, load rating, speed rating and other requirements.

Where are the prices and installation options?

They are deliberately left out until supplier prices, service rates and installation arrangements are confirmed. This demo makes no geographic or availability claims.

Does this replace the WordPress site?

No. This standalone prototype is developed separately. WordPress, WooCommerce and the unpublished WordPress theme remain unchanged by these demo edits.
